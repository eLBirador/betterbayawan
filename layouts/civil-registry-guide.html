Requesting certified copies

Complete the request form. The published checklist asks the adult document owner for a PhilID and a photocopy; a parent may request for a minor. An authorized representative must bring the authorization letter and the required IDs of the concerned party and representative. Ask the Civil Registrar about accepted alternative identification.

  1. Submit the request and identification for record verification.
  2. Pay the assessed fee after the record is located.
  3. Present the official receipt and collect the certified copy.

Marriage-license application

One or both applicants must reside in Bayawan City. The Charter lists birth certificates, CENOMAR, PhilIDs, residency evidence, and pre-marriage counseling certification. Additional documents apply to foreign nationals and dissolved previous marriages.

Parental consent: ages 18–20. Parental advice: ages 21–25, as stated in the published Charter. The license is valid for 120 days from issuance.

A CENOMAR alone does not establish that a previous marriage was legally dissolved.

Marriage registration

Submit the accomplished Certificate of Marriage within 15 days of marriage, or within 30 days for marriages exempt from the license requirement. The contracting parties, solemnizing officer, or church liaison may transact under the Charter.

Death registration

Report the death within 30 calendar days. Bring the accomplished death certificate from the hospital or clinic. For deaths outside a hospital or clinic, the Charter lists barangay captain certification. City Health Office and embalmer review/signatures are separate steps before registration.

Delayed registration and corrections

Requirements depend on the event, applicant’s age, and legal basis. Use the applicable checklist in the Charter; request the current filing assessment before paying. Publication, posting, PSA processing, and court actions can add separate costs and waiting periods.
