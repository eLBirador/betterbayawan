## Checks added

Source-linked financial arithmetic and barangay totals; internal links and assets;
canonical metadata and structured data; dictionary coverage; route aliases;
mobile overflow; navigation, search, language persistence, and financial period
selection. Browser checks are regression checks, not certification of legal or
medical accuracy or universal accessibility conformance.
