Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to digital accessibility for all citizens

WCAG 2.1 Level AA Conformant

Our Commitment

Better Bayawan is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

Accessibility Features

Keyboard Navigation

Keyboard support is provided for navigation and controls; report barriers you encounter.

Screen Reader Support

Uses semantic HTML and accessible labels; assistive-technology testing is ongoing.

Text Alternatives

Informative images should include descriptive alternatives.

Color Contrast

Contrast is reviewed against WCAG AA targets.

Responsive Design

Works on all devices and screen sizes.

No Time Limits

No time limits on reading or interacting.

Known Limitations

  • Some PDF documents may not be fully accessible to screen readers
  • Some third-party embedded content may have accessibility issues

Alternative Access

If you encounter difficulty accessing any information, contact us:

volunteer@BetterBayawan.org

Technical Specifications

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript ARIA

Our Promise

Better Bayawan is committed to ensuring that our digital services are accessible to all citizens, regardless of ability. We view accessibility not as a feature, but as a fundamental right.
