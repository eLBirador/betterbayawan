Introduction

BetterBayawan.org is an independent community information site. This notice describes the website’s storage and external services. Government applications and identity documents should be submitted only through the responsible agency’s official channels.

Legal Basis

Personal information must be processed under a lawful basis appropriate to its purpose under the Philippine Data Privacy Act. The project’s civic purpose does not by itself authorize unrestricted collection or use of personal information.

Information We Collect

The site stores your selected language in local storage on your device. Government service searches run in the browser. If you email the maintainers, your address and message are received through the email provider. Hosting and external services may process technical request information such as IP address and browser details.

The Bayawan quiz saves an unfinished round in session storage and a personal best score in local storage on your device. Quiz answers and scores are not uploaded. Clear this site’s browser storage to remove them. Sharing a result is optional and uses your browser’s sharing or clipboard feature.

How Information Is Used

Language preferences improve navigation. Correspondence is used to answer enquiries and investigate reported issues. Do not send identity documents, financial credentials, or medical records to the volunteer website.

Local Storage and External Services

This build does not load a first-party analytics script. It loads external fonts and icon styles, obtains weather and exchange-rate information, and may embed maps or Facebook content. Those providers may receive technical request information and apply their own cookies or storage. Browser settings can restrict these services; some optional features may then be unavailable.

Data Sharing

The public site does not provide a government application-upload service. Information voluntarily emailed to the maintainers is handled through the email provider and may be used for the requested follow-up. External links and embeds operate under their providers’ terms.

Security

Transport security, hosting controls, dependency maintenance, and contributor review help protect the site. The project does not claim independent security certification or a fixed security-audit schedule. Report a vulnerability through the repository’s security reporting guidance.

Data Retention

The project does not promise automatic deletion after a fixed number of days. Hosting-log retention follows the hosting provider’s configuration. Email correspondence may remain with the email provider for follow-up. Contact the maintainers to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. You can remove the language preference by clearing this site’s browser storage.

Your Privacy Rights

You may ask about personal information held by the maintainers and request access, correction, objection, or deletion subject to applicable law. Send the request to volunteer@BetterBayawan.org. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission through privacy.gov.ph.

Children’s Privacy

Children should not send personal documents or sensitive information to this volunteer website. A parent or guardian can contact the maintainers about information a child has voluntarily sent.

Third-Party Services

Government portals, payment providers, external directories, maps, weather services, fonts, and social-media embeds have their own privacy policies. Review those policies before transacting or providing information.

Policy Changes

This notice should be updated whenever deployed storage, providers, or processing practices change. Last reviewed October 21, 2026.

Contact

For website privacy enquiries: volunteer@BetterBayawan.org. For government transaction records, contact the agency that received the information. The National Privacy Commission’s current contact directory is available at privacy.gov.ph.
